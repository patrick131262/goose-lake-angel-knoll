# Goose Lake

A sunlit farm lake where ten goose breeds each carry one topic about Angels Knoll (Bunker Hill Parcel Y-1, 361 S. Hill St, downtown Los Angeles). Click a flock to read its records; every fact links to its source and says how strong that source is.

Made for the "Three Powers, One Architecture" studio at SCI-Arc, Fall 2026.

## Controls

- Click a flock or its label: read its records
- Drag: orbit · Shift+drag or WASD: pan · Scroll or pinch: zoom
- Light button: morning, afternoon or golden hour
- Three easter eggs: look on the island and in the goose houses

## Notes

- Breed notes are general farm knowledge, not claims about the site.
- Research current as of September 28, 2026.
