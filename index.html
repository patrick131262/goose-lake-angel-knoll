Why is this hill still empty?

  1. The third California Plaza tower died with the early-1990s office market. The slope wasn't the cause.
  2. Angels Landing won staff-level permits in 2022 but never a Council-approved deal. The City ended it in 2024, and the developer is suing.
  3. The land now moves under state surplus rules: affordable developers get the first chance, and any housing must be at least 25% affordable.
  4. As of September 2026, no notice had been filed with the state and no bidder had been named.
